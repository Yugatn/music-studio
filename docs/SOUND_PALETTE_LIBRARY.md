# Sound palette library

This document defines the first reusable musical directions for Yugatn Music Studio. A palette is a starting point for composition, not a fixed genre or a guarantee of a listener's emotional response. Every element remains editable.

## Design principles

- Describe musical behavior, arrangement and production rather than relying on genre labels alone.
- Keep the source composition intact when generating variations.
- Treat references as descriptive context, not a requirement to imitate a specific recording.
- Instrumental output is the default for meditation and cinematic palettes unless vocals are explicitly requested.
- Use contrast, register, density, timing and harmonic tension to shape development instead of making every section louder.
- Store prompts alongside structured intent so a future provider can adapt them without changing the project format.

## Initial palettes

### Shamanic cinematic ritual

**Core identity:** dominant deep frame drums, hypnotic jaw harp or vargan, sparse guitar, low cinematic strings and atmospheric drones.

**Arrangement:** establish space, introduce the drum pulse, develop an ostinato, add answering melodic phrases, build to one earned peak, then remove layers gradually.

**Production:** organic transients, human timing, deep but controlled low end, wide space without masking the central pulse.

**Avoid:** lead vocals by default, busy pop drums, glossy EDM drops and continuous maximum density.

### Dark matrix ambient

**Core identity:** slow sub-bass pulse, restrained granular textures, analog-style drones, metallic resonances and sparse ritual percussion.

**Arrangement:** begin almost empty; use subtle timbral change, filtering and silence as structural events. Let upper layers drift around a stable pulse.

**Production:** low brightness, controlled sub-bass, long transitions and deliberate negative space.

**Avoid:** random horror stingers, bright pop leads and festival-style drops.

### Existential guitar cinema

**Core identity:** a memorable guitar motif supported by cello, restrained violin, bass and selected low-drum accents.

**Arrangement:** state a simple motif, transform it through harmony and register, build toward a restrained peak, then return to a reduced opening phrase.

**Production:** expressive phrasing, audible dynamics and enough space for each instrument to answer the others.

**Avoid:** constant shredding, forced happy resolutions and sentimental stock-music clichés.

## Prompt construction

A useful generation prompt should identify:

1. Instrumental or vocal policy.
2. Dominant instrument and rhythmic role.
3. Supporting instruments and their relationships.
4. Tempo feel and groove.
5. Emotional direction as an artistic target, not a promised effect.
6. Arrangement arc.
7. Mix and performance character.
8. Elements to avoid.

## Implementation status

- **OBSERVED:** the three initial palettes are represented as typed Swift values in CompositionPresets.
- **NOT_TESTED:** the prompts have not been validated against a specific external audio-generation service in this repository.
- **NOT_TESTED:** no audio output is generated by the preset library itself; it currently supplies reusable intent and prompt text to future UI/provider integration.
